How to play

1. Wait

Press the pad. It turns red. Keep still until it changes.

2. Tap on green

The moment you see green, tap, click or press Space. Your time appears in milliseconds.

3. Don’t jump early

Tapping before green is a false start. The round repeats and costs you points.

What is a good reaction time?

Most people react to a visual signal in about 200 to 300 milliseconds. Under 200 ms is very quick. Over 400 ms often means tiredness, distraction or a slow screen or connection.

Average Rating
Under 200 ms Lightning
200–250 ms Sharp
250–320 ms Typical
320–450 ms Steady
Over 450 ms Warming up

Tips to react faster

  • Rest your finger on the pad and keep your eyes on its center.
  • Play when rested. Reaction time slows when you are tired.
  • Use a device with a fast screen. Phones, laptops and monitors add different delays, so compare scores on the same device.
  • Do not guess. A false start costs more than a slightly slow tap.

Questions

Is it free, and do I need an account?

Free, with no account. Everything runs in your browser.

Where are my scores saved?

In your browser’s local storage on this device only. Clearing site data removes them.

Is this scientifically accurate?

It is a fun estimate. Screen refresh, device and browser add small delays, so use it to track your own progress, not as a medical test.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. The whole pad is a large tap target and works on phones, tablets and computers.
